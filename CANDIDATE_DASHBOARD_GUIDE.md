# Candidate Dashboard User Guide

## Overview
The Candidate Dashboard provides a centralized hub for managing your job search, tracking applications, and discovering new opportunities. Monitor your progress and stay organized throughout your job search journey.

## Accessing Your Dashboard

### Prerequisites
1. Candidate account created
2. Logged in with candidate credentials
3. Active internet connection

### How to Access
1. Login with your candidate account
2. Navigate to `/candidate/dashboard` or click "My Dashboard" in navigation
3. Your personalized dashboard loads with all your information

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Header Section
Displays personalized welcome message with quick action button.

**Elements:**
- Personalized greeting: "Welcome, [Your Name]!"
- Dashboard tagline: "Track your job applications and progress"
- "Browse Jobs" button for quick access to job listings

### 2. Profile Completion Progress

Shows how complete your profile is with visual progress tracking.

#### Progress Calculation:
- Name: 20%
- Email: 20%
- Phone: 15%
- Skills: 20%
- Profile Completion: 25%
- **Total: 100%**

#### Visual Indicators:
- **Progress Bar**: Green gradient bar showing completion percentage
- **Percentage**: Numerical value displayed (0-100%)
- **Status Message**: 
  - "Your profile is complete!" (at 100%)
  - "Complete your profile to improve job matching" (below 100%)

#### Actions:
- Click **"Complete Profile"** button to:
  - Add/update personal information
  - Upload resume
  - Add skills
  - Complete other profile fields

### 3. Statistics Cards

Four key metrics displayed in colorful cards:

#### Total Applications
- Shows total number of jobs you've applied for
- Gradient purple background
- Quick reference for your activity level

#### Shortlisted
- Count of jobs where you've been selected for next round
- Green gradient background
- Positive achievement indicator

#### Under Review
- Applications currently being reviewed by recruiters
- Pink gradient background
- Indicates active interest from companies

#### Not Selected
- Applications that were rejected
- Orange gradient background
- Learning opportunity indicator

### 4. Resume Section

Manage and monitor your resume status.

#### If Resume Uploaded:
- Green checkmark icon indicating successful upload
- "Resume Uploaded" label
- Last updated date display
- **"Download Resume"** button to save a copy

#### If No Resume:
- File icon with message
- "No resume uploaded yet"
- **"Upload Resume"** button linking to profile completion

#### Use Cases:
- Ensure resume is available for applications
- Download for backup
- Check upload date
- Verify file is ready

### 5. Top Skills Section

Display your professional skills extracted from resume.

#### Features:
- Shows top 10 skills from your parsed resume
- Skills displayed as colorful badges with spark icons
- Only visible if skills are present in resume
- Automatically extracted from uploaded resume

#### Use:
- Quick skill overview
- See what you listed in resume
- Identify any missing skills
- Share with recruiters

### 6. Recent Applications

Track your recent job applications with status.

#### Information Displayed:
- **Job Title**: Position you applied for
- **Company Name**: Company offering the job
- **Applied Date**: When you submitted application
- **Status Badge**: Current status with color coding:
  - Blue: Applied (submitted)
  - Yellow: Reviewing (under consideration)
  - Green: Shortlisted (moving forward)
  - Red: Rejected (not selected)
  - Cyan: Accepted (offer received)

#### Actions:
- Click **"View All"** to see complete application history
- See up to 5 recent applications on dashboard
- Full application tracking in dedicated page

#### Navigation:
- Link to view all applications with more details
- Status tracking over time
- Application response timeline

### 7. Recommended Jobs For You

AI-powered job recommendations tailored to your profile.

#### Job Card Information:
- **Job Title**: Position name
- **Match Score**: AI-calculated compatibility (%)
- **Company Name**: Hiring company
- **Location**: Job location
- **Job Type**: Full-time, Part-time, etc.
- **Salary**: Expected salary range

#### Features:
- Shows up to 5 most relevant jobs
- Match score indicates fit with your profile
- Jobs update based on your profile completeness
- Click card to view full job details

#### Scoring:
- Based on skills match
- Experience level alignment
- Location preferences (if set)
- Education requirements

### 8. Next Steps Section

Personalized action items based on your profile state.

#### Dynamic Recommendations:

**1. Complete Your Profile** (if <100% complete)
- Status: Recommended
- Why: Better job matching and recruiter visibility
- Action: **"Complete"** button
- Leads to: Profile completion page

**2. Apply to More Jobs** (if <5 applications)
- Status: Recommended
- Why: More applications = more opportunities
- Action: **"Browse"** button
- Leads to: Job search page

**3. Prepare for Interviews** (if no shortlisted applications)
- Status: Recommended
- Why: Get ready for opportunities
- Action: **"Prepare"** button
- Leads to: AI interview preparation

**4. Check Messages** (always shown)
- Status: Always available
- Why: Stay connected with recruiters
- Action: **"Messages"** button
- Leads to: Messaging interface

#### Color Scheme:
- Numbered indicators (1, 2, 3, 4)
- Purple gradient backgrounds
- Clear call-to-action buttons

## Common Tasks

### Task 1: Complete Your Profile
1. View profile completion percentage
2. Click **"Complete Profile"** button
3. Fill in missing information:
   - Personal details
   - Resume upload
   - Skills
   - Education
   - Experience
4. Save changes
5. Progress bar updates automatically

### Task 2: View Your Applications
1. Scroll to "Recent Applications" section
2. Review recent applications (5 shown)
3. See job title, company, date, status
4. Click **"View All"** to see complete history

### Task 3: Check Job Match Score
1. Scroll to "Recommended For You" section
2. Look at match score percentage on job cards
3. Higher % = better fit for your profile
4. Click job to see detailed match analysis

### Task 4: Download Your Resume
1. Find "Resume" section
2. Check if resume is uploaded (green checkmark)
3. Click **"Download Resume"** button
4. File saves to your downloads

### Task 5: Apply to Recommended Jobs
1. Find "Recommended For You" section
2. Review job cards with match scores
3. Click job card to view full details
4. Click **"Apply Now"** button
5. Application submitted to company

## FAQ

### Q: How often does my dashboard update?
A: Dashboard updates in real-time when you refresh or navigate between sections.

### Q: Why aren't jobs showing in recommendations?
A: Recommendations improve as your profile becomes more complete. Add more skills and details.

### Q: Can I change my application status?
A: No, status changes are controlled by recruiters. You can only track status.

### Q: How do I withdraw an application?
A: Contact the recruiter directly via messages to withdraw.

### Q: Why is my match score low?
A: Match score depends on profile completeness and skills. Complete your profile to improve.

### Q: How long does a profile review take?
A: Typically 24-48 hours. Some companies may take longer.

### Q: Can I apply to the same job twice?
A: No, duplicate applications are prevented. Your first application stands.

### Q: How do I contact recruiters?
A: Use the Messages section to send inquiries to recruiters.
